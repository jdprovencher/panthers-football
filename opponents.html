Worcester State MASCAC

Lance Williams

Senior · Wide Receiver · Westerly, R.I.

Torched Plymouth State for seven catches, 152 yards and touchdowns of 42 and 44 yards in the Lancers' 28–6 win on Sept. 12. The reigning first-team All-MASCAC wideout was named MASCAC Offensive Player of the Week.

MASCAC Off. POTW (9/14)
Bridgewater State MASCAC

Jayden Barber

Freshman · Quarterback · Harwich, Mass.

A three-time MASCAC Rookie of the Week. Against the Panthers on Sept. 19 he threw for 178 yards and a score and ran for 63 yards and two more touchdowns in a 35–7 Bears win.

3× MASCAC Rookie of the Week
Dean College MASCAC

Herman Esquivel Mendez

Senior · Linebacker · Chelsea, Mass.

A bright spot for a Bulldogs defense still searching for its first win: eight tackles, six of them solo, plus a tackle for loss in Dean's early-season meeting with Mass. Maritime.

Defensive standout
Mass. Maritime MASCAC

Jackson Mahoney

Senior · Linebacker · Falmouth, Mass.

The reigning MASCAC Defensive Player of the Year stuffed the stat sheet with 13 tackles, a tackle for loss, a key pass breakup and a blocked kick in a 26–22 win over UMass Dartmouth.

MASCAC Def. POTW (10/5)
Westfield State MASCAC

Malachi Hymes

First Year · Wide Receiver · Hartford, Conn.

Enjoyed a breakout day with four catches for 166 yards and touchdowns of 61 and 46 yards in a 26–8 win over Dean, earning MASCAC Rookie of the Week honors. He also scored twice against Bridgewater State.

MASCAC Rookie (9/21)
Framingham State MASCAC

Michael Marcucella

Sophomore · Quarterback · Foxboro, Mass.

Completed 21-of-30 passes for 341 yards and three touchdowns — of 30, 55 and 16 yards — in a 23–21 win over UMass Dartmouth. Named MASCAC Offensive Player of the Week, he has the Rams unbeaten and atop the league.

MASCAC Off. POTW (9/21)
Fitchburg State MASCAC

Dasha Domercant

Junior · Quarterback · Bellingham, Mass.

Opened the season with a do-it-all performance: 191 passing yards and two scoring strikes plus a pair of rushing touchdowns in a 35–26 win over Dean. Named MASCAC Offensive Player of the Week.

MASCAC Off. POTW (9/8)
UMass Dartmouth MASCAC

Mekhi Wilson

Senior · Running Back · Narragansett, R.I.

Rumbled for a season-high 211 yards on a career-best 27 carries with touchdowns of 76 and 16 yards at Mass. Maritime. The senior captain leads the conference in rushes, rushing yards and yards per game.

MASCAC Off. POTW (10/5)
New England College Non-Conf.

Conrado Lago

Wide Receiver · Pilgrims

In the 2026 season opener at Panther Field, Lago hauled in an 18-yard touchdown pass from Aidan Hughes as the Pilgrims scored twice in the fourth quarter of a 28–14 loss to Plymouth State.
