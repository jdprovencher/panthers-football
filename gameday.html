The Home of the Panthers

Panther Field

The Panthers play their home games at Panther Field in Plymouth, New Hampshire, with a capacity of roughly 1,200. It's an intimate, old-school New England college football setting where the crowd sits close to the action.

Panther Field has been the program's home in two eras: the original grounds from 1970 to 1982, and again since 2021. In between, from 1983 to 2020, the Panthers played at Currier Field.
