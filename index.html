The Program

Who are the Panthers?

Plymouth State University football represents Plymouth, New Hampshire at the NCAA Division III level. Fielded since 1970, the Panthers wear green and white and compete in the Massachusetts State Collegiate Athletic Conference (MASCAC), which they joined in 2013.

From Jay Cottone's unbeaten 1982 squad to the 2022 New England Bowl championship and a shared 2025 MASCAC regular-season title, Plymouth State football has a long tradition of hard-nosed, run-first New England football.
