Team & Coaches

A hard-nosed, downhill running team built in New England. Meet the staff and the players setting the tone for the Panthers in 2026.

The Staff

Coaching leadership

PC

Paul Castonia

Head Coach · since 2003

The longest-serving coach in program history, Castonia has guided Plymouth State to MASCAC titles and was named the league's Coach of the Year for the third time in 2025.

DZ

Devin Zeman

Co-Head Coach · since 2020

Elevated to co-head coach in 2020, Zeman helps direct a program that reached the 2022 New England Bowl and shared the 2025 MASCAC regular-season crown.

Athletic Director: Kim Bownes. For the full staff directory, visit PSU Athletics.
