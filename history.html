Program History

More than half a century of Plymouth State football — 14 conference championships, five trips to the NCAA Division III playoffs, and a running back who crashed the Heisman Trophy conversation.

Through the years

Key moments

1970

Varsity football arrives

The Panthers play their first varsity season under Walter Murphy. Laconia's Alan Wool returns the opening kickoff 95 yards for the program's first touchdown in a 41–13 loss to Nichols before nearly 3,000 fans at D&M Park.

1981–1985

The Jay Cottone dynasty

Cottone guides one of the great small-college runs in New England, winning five straight conference titles behind an unbeaten 10–0 campaign in 1982 and back-to-back 10–1 seasons. The 1984 team reached the NCAA playoffs.

1985

Joe Dudek crashes the Heisman race

Panthers running back Joe Dudek finishes ninth in Heisman Trophy voting — an extraordinary result for a Division III player — and is later inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame.

1986–1992

Desloges keeps it rolling

Lou Desloges adds four more conference championships and three division titles, with ECAC postseason appearances throughout the late 1980s and early 1990s.

1994

First NCAA playoff win

Under Don Brown, Plymouth State captures the Freedom Football Conference title and beats Merchant Marine 19–18 in the first round of the NCAA Division III playoffs — the program's first postseason victory.

2008

Back on the national stage

Paul Castonia's Panthers go 10–2 and 7–0 in the New England Football Conference, earning a place in the NCAA playoffs.

2013

A new conference home

Plymouth State joins the Massachusetts State Collegiate Athletic Conference (MASCAC), renewing natural rivalries across New England and Massachusetts.

2017

MASCAC co-champions

A 9–2 season delivers a share of the MASCAC title and a trip to the NCAA Division III playoffs.

2022

New England Bowl champions

The Panthers finish 9–2 and win the New England Bowl, 21–20, capping one of the best seasons of the modern era.

2025

Share of the MASCAC crown

Plymouth State goes 8–2 (7–2 MASCAC), shares the regular-season conference title, finishes No. 9 in the final New England Division III poll, and sees Paul Castonia named MASCAC Coach of the Year.

Legends

Notable Panthers

Joe Dudek

One of the most decorated players in Division III history. The star running back finished ninth in 1985 Heisman Trophy voting and was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame.

Don Brown

Coached the Panthers to back-to-back Freedom Football Conference titles in 1994–95 before building a long career as a defensive coordinator at the FBS level, including UMass and Michigan.

Jay Cottone & Lou Desloges

Between them, these two coaches delivered the bulk of Plymouth State's conference championships and its 1980s run of New England dominance.
